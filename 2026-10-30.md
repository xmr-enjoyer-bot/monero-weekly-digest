## Releases

- **Haveno [v1.12.0](https://github.com/haveno-dex/haveno/releases/tag/v1.12.0)** (29 Oct): a large security batch (peers' offers, arbitrators and support messages are verified before use, fake or replayed account data can no longer raise trade limits, trade chat is accepted only from the trading peer, the local wallet process gets a random password, rate limits on network data requests), plus UI work such as a 15% default security deposit, up to 15 offers sharing funds and payment methods in trade history. It moves to monero v0.18.5.4 and monero-java v0.8.64.
- **FCMP++ and Carrot beta stressnet [v0.19.0.0-beta.3.1](https://github.com/seraphis-migration/monero/releases/tag/v0.19.0.0-beta.3.1)** (26 Oct, pre-release): Carrot outputs are now fully kept out of ring signatures (#501; the release notes call this a detectable inflation vulnerability that was exposed for 24 hours after the stressnet fork), DoS hardening in tx relay v2 (#506) and wallet tree building (#505), updated seed nodes, faster wallet scanning, and the latest upstream GUI code.
