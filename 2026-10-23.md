## Releases

- **Monero CLI and GUI [v0.18.5.4](https://github.com/monero-project/monero/releases/tag/v0.18.5.4)** published 17 Oct. A minor bug-fix release: the daemon's DNSSEC validation for checkpoints, blocklists and update checks is fixed ([#11542](https://github.com/monero-project/monero/pull/11542)), and string-to-integer conversion in JSON RPC requests is restored, to be removed in v0.19 as a breaking change ([#11552](https://github.com/monero-project/monero/pull/11552)). getmonero.org added the downloads and release notes ([monero-site#2729](https://github.com/monero-project/monero-site/pull/2729), [#2728](https://github.com/monero-project/monero-site/pull/2728)).
- **Haveno [v1.11.0](https://github.com/haveno-dex/haveno/releases/tag/v1.11.0)** (17 Oct): Tor 0.4.9.14 with high-severity security fixes, Jackson and Logback updates, a fix for arbitrators dropping simultaneous offer-signing requests from one maker (offers timed out after 180 s), IPv6 Monero nodes without Tor, a redesigned network settings page with editable nodes, and SEPA QR codes for buyers.
